All code for Henry Island has been built, tested, committed, and deployed directly to your GitHub repository and Azure Static Web Apps: 👉 GitHub Repo: https://github.com/Gandalf1976/henry-island
 👉 Live Website: https://yellow-flower-03642c410.3.azurestaticapps.net

🌟 What's New in this Major Update
1. 🌐 Real-Time Live Cloud Sync (No Manual Sharing Needed!)
Automatic Sync: Scores, completed daily games, and messages automatically synchronize in real time across all family phones, tablets, and computers.
Instant Updates: When a family member completes a game on their phone, everyone's podium and snowmen update live within milliseconds.
Celebration Toasts: Family members receive gentle notification banners and festive holiday jingles whenever someone finishes a daily game.
Live Indicator: A glowing green Live Cloud Sync 🟢 indicator in the header lets everyone know the connection is active.
2. 📱 Device Identity Lock ("Who are you?")
First-Visit Picker: When a family member first opens the website on their phone, they are greeted by a friendly dialog asking: "Who are you?".
Remembered Automatically: Their chosen identity is saved to their browser/phone storage (localStorage). They will never have to re-select themselves every day.
Easy Switching: On shared devices (e.g. an iPad), simply click the Avatar button in the header and tap "Switch Device Player" at any time.
3. 👥 Player Management (Add, Edit & Delete)
Accessible from the header "Manage" button or inside the Avatar profile card.
Edit Names & Avatars: Change existing player names and switch between 6 holiday avatars.
Add Family & Friends: Easily add new family members or visiting friends at any time.
Remove Players: Safely delete players who are no longer participating.
4. 🎮 Full 3D Animal Crossing Winter World (Three.js)

The 2D canvas has been upgraded to a rich, expansive 3D Three.js Island:

Bigger Scale & Buildings:
🟩 Wordle Lodge: Timber-framed log cottage with snow-covered roof, brick chimney, and cozy windows.
🪷 Frozen Lake: Translucent frozen blue ice rink with wooden observation deck and lamp posts.
🧠 Quiz Lodge: Cozy alpine chalet where the 60-second quiz is hosted.
⛄ Town Square Podium: 3D elevated podium with dynamic snowmen representing 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
📌 Bulletin Board: Rustic wooden corkboard notice board in the village center.
🎅 Santa's Sleigh & Reindeer: Santa in his big red sleigh loaded with gifts and Rudolph with his glowing red nose.
🐧 Waddling Penguins: Animated 3D penguins that waddle across the snowy paths.
❄️ 3D Drifting Snow: Thousands of soft 3D snowflakes gently falling over the island.
🎄 Giant Christmas Trees: Evergreen fir trees with colorful baubles and glowing golden star toppers.
Smooth Walking & Camera Follow:
The third-person camera smoothly tracks your character as you explore the island.
Controls: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys to move; Spacebar or click/tap action prompt to enter buildings.
Mobile Touch: Virtual joystick appears automatically on phones and tablets.
Quick-Travel Dock: Teleport instantly to any lodge or landmark with the bottom dock buttons.
5. 💬 Family Bulletin Board & Notifications
Tap the "💬 Messages" button in the header or walk up to the 3D Bulletin Board in Town Square.
Live Holiday Notes: Post greetings, friendly challenges, and holiday banter to the whole family.
Holiday Emoji Bar: One-tap quick inserts for 🎄, 🎅, ⛄, 🎁, ❄️, 🦌, 🍪, ✨, ⭐.
Unread Notification Badge: A red counter in the top bar alerts family members whenever someone posts a new message.
